Add `dexto projects` to manage Dexto Projects from the terminal: `list`, `get`, `create`, `update`, `delete`, `move` (a chat into or out of a Project), `start` (a Project chat from a brief) and `run` (message the Project's coordinator). Every command takes `--json`; commands that change something ask first, or take `--yes`.
